## Rules

### A badge's colour is a status, and its words say the same

A badge takes one of the five status tones, and its text names the status as well, so the colour is never the only thing that carries it. Neutral is for a label or a count that marks nothing good or bad.

### A badge holds a count or a word or two

A badge is a label pinned to something else: a number of unread items, or a short status such as "New". Anything longer is a sentence, and belongs in the text beside it.

### Subtle is the default, and solid is for the one badge that must be found

The subtle tint sits quietly in a list of many. The solid variant swaps the tone's own pair, so it stands out without adding a colour; keep it for the badge the reader has to find first. The outline variant is for a badge on a surface that already carries a tint.

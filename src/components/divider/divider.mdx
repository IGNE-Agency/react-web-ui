## Rules

### A divider is the last resort for telling two groups apart

Space separates two groups before a line does, and a surface tier before that. Reach for a divider only where a gap is not enough, such as between groups of the same kind in one long list.

### A divider's subtitle names what follows it

A subtitle under the rule says what the next group is, such as "Earlier messages", in the small muted body text. A divider with nothing to name has no subtitle.

## Rules

### An avatar stands beside the name, never in place of it

An avatar is a picture of someone whose name is already on the screen next to it, so it is hidden from assistive technology rather than read out twice. Where there is no room for the name, the name goes in a tooltip or a list, as `AvatarGroup` does, and the avatar still says nothing.

### A person keeps one colour, and it is never a status colour

Without a portrait, an avatar shows the initials over a colour worked out from the name, so the same person has the same colour on every screen. The colours are the identity tones and the brand tint, never green, amber or red, which would read as a status.

### A portrait that fails falls back to the initials

While a portrait loads, and when it fails, the initials show in its place, so an avatar never shows a broken image or an empty circle.

### The prefab is the parts, composed

`Avatar` takes a `name` and a `src` and composes the parts hanging off it: `Root` is the circle in the name's colour, `Image` the portrait, and `Fallback` what shows in its place, the initials. Compose the parts by hand where the fallback needs other content.

```tsx
<Avatar.Root name="Ada Lovelace">
	<Avatar.Image src={colleague.portraitUrl} />
	<Avatar.Fallback>AL</Avatar.Fallback>
</Avatar.Root>
```

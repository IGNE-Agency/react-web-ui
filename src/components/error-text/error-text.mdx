## Rules

### Errors that arrive together drop in one after another

Each message drops into place, held back 20 ms for every message ahead of it that arrived in the same frame. Inside a `Form.Root`, the root counts them across the whole form, so the errors of a refused submit cascade down the page in document order. An error that arrives on its own, such as one that follows a correction, starts its own cascade at once. Outside a form each block cascades on its own.

### The text is there from the first frame, and motion is optional

The messages are in the page, and in the accessibility tree, from the first render: an alert announces them at once, whatever the motion does. The drop-in only runs for someone who hasn't asked for less motion.

### Error text is one message per line, or nothing

`ErrorText` takes one message or a list, skips empty entries, and renders nothing at all when none are left, so it can stay in place while a field is valid. In a field, use `Field.Error`, which normalises the field's errors and makes them its live region.

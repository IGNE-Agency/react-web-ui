## Rules

### Each item is a heading, at the level the page needs

Every item's trigger sits in a heading, so a screen reader can list the items and jump to one by name. The heading is an `h3` unless the page's outline needs another level; only the page knows its own outline, so the call site sets it.

### One item open at a time, unless the reader compares them

An accordion opens one item and shuts the one before it, so the stack stays short. Let several stay open only where the reader reads two side by side.

### Items fold the way a collapsible does

An item's trigger and panel are a collapsible's: a label that names what the panel holds, a chevron that turns over, and a panel that grows along its height for those who want motion.

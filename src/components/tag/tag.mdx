## Rules

### A tag leads with at most one indicator

A tag leads with at most one indicator. A dot is enough where the tone is a distinction between tags a reader is comparing — a column of statuses, where the label is already there and the dot only speeds up the scan. Where the tone is the message rather than a distinction, that is a glyph: a table whose every row is an error has no contrasting tag to compare against, so the tone carries the meaning by itself and would be lost on anyone who cannot see it.

## Rules

### A region's first wait shows late, and is announced once

A loading message is for a region waiting for its first content. It shows nothing for the first moment, so a quick load does not flicker, and then shows a moving indicator and its title, announced once as a status. A region that already shows content and refreshes it uses `Stale` instead, and a wait whose shape is known is drawn as skeletons.

### The indicator moves, and may be the app's own mark

The indicator is a spinner unless the app passes its own, such as its animated logo, through `illustration`. Whatever it is, it keeps changing, so a slow wait never looks like a stuck one.

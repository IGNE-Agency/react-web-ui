## Rules

### A checkbox fills with the brand colour when checked

A checkbox is a squared control with [a hairline border](?path=/docs/design-system-principles-separation--docs#the-four-cases-that-justify-a-line) that fills with the brand colour when checked, and the tick scales in. Hovering its label warms the box's border.

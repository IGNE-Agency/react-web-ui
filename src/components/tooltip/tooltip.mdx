## Rules

### A tooltip is dark ink on a light surface

A tooltip is dark ink on a light surface, [like every other popup](?path=/docs/design-system-patterns--docs#every-popup-floats-on-the-overlay-surface-at-the-first-level-with-no-outline), not an inverted black chip. An inverted tooltip is a second, contradictory visual language for "floating thing", and it has to be [re-inverted by hand for a dark theme](?path=/docs/design-system-foundation-colour--docs#a-theme-re-points-the-roles-and-touches-nothing-else).

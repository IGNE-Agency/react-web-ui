## Rules

### Stale content stays on screen while it refreshes

A region that already shows content keeps it while a refresh runs in the background, rather than swapping it for a loading message. A refresh that finishes quickly shows nothing at all.

### The longer the wait, the more it says

The content dims once the refresh has taken a moment, and is marked busy. Once the refresh is slow, an overlay covers it with a moving indicator and a line of copy, announced as a status, and the content goes inert rather than disabled. Once it looks stuck, the overlay offers a retry.

### The indicator may be the app's own mark

The overlay's indicator is a spinner unless the app passes its own, such as its animated logo, through `illustration`.

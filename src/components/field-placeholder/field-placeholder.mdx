## Rules

### A field whose code is on its way holds its place

A field the app loads on first use renders `FieldPlaceholder` until its code arrives. It keeps the label's line and the control's height, so the form does not jump when the real field takes its place, and it varies between one control and a split pair, so a column of placeholders does not read as a pattern.

### A placeholder says nothing

The placeholder is hidden from assistive technology. The region it sits in announces the wait once.

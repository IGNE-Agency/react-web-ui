## Rules

### An expanded rail is wide enough for its labels

An expanded rail is wide enough for its labels. [No ellipsis](?path=/docs/design-system-foundation-type--docs#text-that-does-not-fit-is-folded-never-mangled), and no hint to recover lost text: size the rail from the longest label, and see [a panel opens to fit its content, not to a guess](?path=/docs/design-system-principles-layout--docs#a-panel-opens-to-fit-its-content-not-to-a-guess) for how that survives a collapse animation.

### A collapsed rail names its items with a tooltip

A collapsed rail names its items with [a tooltip on the icon](?path=/docs/design-system-principles-interaction--docs#an-icon-only-control-always-has-a-tooltip-and-an-accessible-name), carrying the same string as the accessible name. An entry with `labelled={false}` does both: `Nav.Root` mounts the one tooltip its entries share, so the hint travels between them rather than one closing as the next opens.

### A bar's entries are sized to their longest word

A bar cannot be widened [the way a rail can](#an-expanded-rail-is-wide-enough-for-its-labels), because its inline size is the device's. [Size its entries to their longest word](?path=/docs/design-system-foundation-type--docs#text-that-does-not-fit-is-folded-never-mangled) instead, and keep the count of destinations low enough that those add up to less than a phone.

### The current page in a bar of tabs is a pill that slides there

A horizontal nav marks the current page the way [tabs](?path=/docs/components-tabs--docs#the-active-tab-is-a-pill-that-slides-between-tabs) mark the open tab: a filled pill that slides between entries. Its links are a router's, so no tab primitive publishes the current one's place; the list measures the entry marked `aria-current="page"` and publishes the same `--active-tab-left` and `--active-tab-width`. The current label takes the inverted ink as the pill arrives.
